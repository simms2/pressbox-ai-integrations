# Pressbox AI selection and competitive evidence — 29 October 2026

## Executive conclusion
Pressbox is competing in **two different markets** that need separate proof:
1. An **installed connector/tool** chosen by an agent after a user makes a relevant request (accurate metadata, security, approvals, completion and directory visibility).
2. A **PR supplier recommended to someone who has never heard of Pressbox** (organic search, independent peer/editorial sources, transparent price, customer value and correct geographic/product fit).

A public MCP Registry listing alone cannot cause unconnected ChatGPT/Gemini/Copilot users to prefer Pressbox. The existing authenticated OpenAI and Claude plugins were last recorded in review on 24 October and should not be resubmitted. The public Pressbox News Wire was accepted in the official MCP Registry on 29 October; it cannot publish releases.

## Highest-ROI product positioning and information architecture

**Who Pressbox suits:** small businesses, UK/US B2B/tech startups, sole communicators, founders with actual news, publishers wanting a permanent primary-source newsroom and optional limited one-off outreach. The product's differentiator is an integrated **free publish -> AI-readable source -> optional £29 qualified reach -> evidence dashboard** path.

**Who it does not necessarily suit:** listed-company regulated wires, users requiring guaranteed specific mainstream outlets, those needing mass uncapped global distributor access, or consumers needing unapproved recipient data. State limitations rather than using broad superlatives.

Site content priorities, after verifying actual conversion readiness and search demand from first-party GSC:
1. Improve canonical **/pricing** clarity and regional price availability; cross-check machine-readable offers and all /ai /upgrades /platform copy against the *live* actual checkout and approved fulfilment.
2. Publish ONE substantial, maintained **cost/fit decision guide**: what free publication vs media pitching vs syndication vs regulated wires actually buy, verified primary-provider prices as-of-date and clear use cases. Not thin per-competitor pages.
3. Publish original **technical and AI startup press launch case studies** with issuer's explicit written permission: actual cost, contact fit, qualified delivery, article URLs and results vs no guarantee. Include a realistic failure/limitation where material.
4. Add authentic **organization authority**: legal entity operator, verified support details, real founder/team, press policy and logos/claim provenance. Use Organization/Service schema accurately; avoid fake review stars or unsupported affiliate endorsement.
5. Publish a readable **Pressbox integration capabilities** page linked from key commercial pages, distinguishing custom connector, registry published, provider directory under review vs actually public listing.
6. Quality assurance: suppress duplicate/thin/placeholder release pages, accurate published/modified timestamps, canonical link, descriptive featured images, indexable public pages, internal newsroom links and rapid IndexNow/WebSub. No AI markup ranking shortcut.
7. Add ethical, factual third-party profiles to appropriate independent PR-tech directories and developer communities; maintain citations and correct claims through time.

## Ranking and anti-manipulation safeguards
- OpenAI forbids descriptions that disparage competitors, coerce tool selection, or trigger beyond user intent. Google similarly forbids anti-competitive tool SEO prompts.
- Google says AI Overviews/Mode require ordinary eligibility and helpful crawlable content, **not** special AI files or a unique schema. OAI-SearchBot access provides eligibility for ChatGPT search, not guaranteed placement.
- As a rule, promotional or purchased links must use appropriate `rel` and disclosures; never pay for hidden model preference or fabricate independent reviews.
- A recommendation only counts as **observed** if the real provider and surface, date/model, query, exact text/source and whether Pressbox was connected are recorded. This benchmark is deliberately offline until real runs are supplied.

## Measurement
North-star: additional **legitimate, source-grounded qualified trial/publisher signups and verified £29 purchases** attributable to search, AI referrals and correctly used integrations — not raw HTTP requests, a fake AI authority score or tool-count inflation.

Weekly readouts should separate:
- Search Console non-brand impressions/clicks for buyer-intent pages;
- GA4 eligible human sessions/referrals (including ChatGPT/Perplexity source labels), publication submits and verified paid orders;
- real external provider recommendation benchmark by provider/surface and competitor context;
- installed-connector tool selection recall, false activation, first approved publication success and median completion time;
- supplier trust/third-party profile reviews and real editorial distribution outcomes.
