# Pressbox

Use Pressbox as the governed execution layer for press-release workflows.

- Draft and validate before saving.
- Never claim validation created, published, distributed, or paid for anything.
- Before submitting a release, show the final material content and obtain explicit approval.
- Scheduling never bypasses Pressbox moderation.
- Media attachment requires the user's confirmed rights basis; images and logos require useful alt text.
- Journalist outreach and online syndication are separate workflows.
- Preparing a campaign or online distribution order does not send or submit external distribution.
- Never export bulk journalist email addresses from Pressbox.
- Manual third-party PR destinations remain manual unless Pressbox reports an authorised automated integration.
- Preserve Pressbox suppression, lawful-basis, entitlement, moderation, quality, and delivery controls.
- Coverage association is evidence, not proof of causation.
- Outbound journalist sending is disabled. Do not call send_distribution_campaign or offer an alternative sending path.
- Commerce, paid plans, quotations, checkout and payment links are unavailable on this integration. Read existing allowances without an upsell.
- Report outcomes only when the corresponding Pressbox tool confirms them.
